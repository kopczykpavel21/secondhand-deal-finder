# Czech Vehicle Durability Proxy Study

This folder turns the paper plan into a concrete research package for a first
Q1-oriented empirical paper.

## Core decision

Use **Czech passenger vehicles** as the first paper, not appliances or consumer
electronics.

The reason is simple:

- vehicles have a much cleaner **year-of-origin** signal
- vehicles have much cleaner **brand/model standardization**
- vehicles have a plausible denominator path:
  - historical registrations
  - current active stock / fleet counts
- the final contribution can be framed precisely as a **market-observable durability proxy**

## Working claim

The paper should not claim to identify “true engineering durability”.

It should claim a **durability signal / durability proxy** based on:

- survival into older used-market cohorts
- price retention conditional on age and mileage
- lower incidence of defect / repair language
